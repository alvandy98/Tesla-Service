Common Tesla concerns

What is your vehicle doing?

You do not need to know the technical name of the problem. Start with the warning, symptom, or behavior you are noticing.

01 CHARGING

Your Tesla will not charge

Charging problems can involve the charge port, low-voltage system, communication faults, high-voltage isolation concerns, software, or onboard charging components.

Common symptoms

  • Unable to charge warning
  • Supercharging unavailable
  • Charging stops unexpectedly
  • Charge port does not respond
  • Vehicle charges unusually slowly

What we inspect

  • Active charging faults
  • Charge-port communication
  • Low-voltage power condition
  • Isolation and high-voltage alerts
  • Related modules and system data

02 ALERTS

Warning messages keep appearing

Tesla alerts can point to a single component, a communication failure, or a larger system condition. The wording alone does not always reveal the root cause.

Common symptoms

  • Vehicle may not restart
  • Power reduced
  • Service required
  • Electrical system warning
  • Multiple alerts appearing together

What we inspect

  • Active and stored fault information
  • Related vehicle systems
  • Voltage and communication conditions
  • Recent repairs or collision history
  • Whether faults return after clearing

03 COMPUTERS

The screen, computer, or modules are not responding

Modern Teslas rely on a network of computers and electronic modules. A failure in one system can create problems across several parts of the vehicle.

Common symptoms

  • Black or restarting touchscreen
  • Modules appear offline
  • Features stop working
  • Vehicle will not fully power on
  • Communication-related alerts

What we inspect

  • Computer and module communication
  • Power and ground conditions
  • Gateway-related faults
  • Network and communication issues
  • Select module setup or programming needs
Request computer-system diagnostics →
04 LOW VOLTAGE

The low-voltage battery keeps draining

A low-voltage battery warning does not always mean the battery itself is the only problem. Charging, power distribution, computers, modules, pumps, and other systems may contribute.

Common symptoms

  • Low-voltage battery warning
  • Vehicle repeatedly shuts down
  • Battery loses charge quickly
  • Vehicle will not wake up
  • Electrical systems behave inconsistently

What we inspect

  • Battery condition and voltage
  • Low-voltage charging behavior
  • Abnormal system loads
  • Power distribution and connections
  • Modules preventing normal sleep
Request low-voltage diagnostics →
05 THERMAL

Cooling pumps, fans, or temperatures seem abnormal

Tesla thermal systems support the battery, drive systems, electronics, cabin climate, and charging. Cooling faults can affect several vehicle systems at once.

Common symptoms

  • Pumps continue running
  • Cooling-system warnings
  • Unusual fan or pump noise
  • High computer temperatures
  • Problems after coolant service or collision repair

What we inspect

  • Cooling and thermal faults
  • Pump operation and communication
  • Coolant levels and circulation concerns
  • Temperature readings
  • Related electrical and module faults
Request cooling-system diagnostics →

07 COLLISION

The vehicle has electrical faults after an accident or repair

Collision damage can affect control modules, crash data, sensors, wiring, power distribution, cooling components, and communication between systems—even after the visible body repairs are complete.

Common symptoms

  • Vehicle will not power on normally
  • Airbag or restraint alerts remain
  • Systems stay offline after parts replacement
  • New faults appear after repair
  • Radar or driver-assistance warnings

What we inspect

  • Crash-related modules and fault information
  • Replacement-module communication
  • Power, wiring, and connector concerns
  • Sensor and radar-related faults
  • Related cooling and low-voltage issues

When to schedule

Do not wait for a small fault to become a larger one.

Some alerts are informational. Others can leave the vehicle unable to restart, charge, or enter drive.

SCHEDULE SOON

The vehicle still drives, but warnings keep returning

Schedule a diagnostic before the vehicle loses additional functionality or the underlying problem worsens.

PRIORITY

The vehicle may not restart or has reduced power

Avoid relying on the vehicle for essential travel until the warning has been investigated.

STOP AND CONTACT US

The vehicle will not charge, enter drive, or remain powered

Contact us with the warning messages and current vehicle condition so we can discuss the safest next step.
