Our journey

Built on electronics. Evolved for Tesla.

ProCode Solutions is a family-run business that has spent nearly ten years adapting alongside increasingly advanced vehicle technology.

01

Where we started

Automotive module repair

We began repairing control modules after vehicle collisions, clearing crash-related codes, and resolving warning-light and communication faults.

02

Expanding capabilities

Radar calibration and advanced electronics

As vehicle technology evolved, we expanded into radar sensor adjustment, calibration, and more advanced electronic-system work.

03

Recognizing a need

Independent Tesla diagnostics

We saw how few independent shops could diagnose electric vehicles and how expensive the available options could be for Tesla owners.

04

The next chapter

Vehicle restoration and sales

After purchasing and restoring our first Teslas, we expanded into professionally prepared vehicle sales while continuing to grow our diagnostic capabilities.
