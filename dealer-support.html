Common shop scenarios

Send us the vehicles slowing down your workflow.

These are examples of situations where a dealer or repair facility may benefit from specialized Tesla diagnostic support.

01

Body repairs are finished, but the vehicle will not enter Drive

The visible collision work is complete, yet high-voltage, restraint, module, interlock, or communication faults remain.

02

Replacement modules are installed but remain offline

The vehicle still shows communication faults or unavailable systems after components have been replaced.

03

The low-voltage battery keeps draining

A battery has already been replaced, but the vehicle continues to lose power, shut down, or remain awake unexpectedly.

04

The vehicle will not charge or Supercharge

Charging equipment appears functional, but the Tesla continues to reject charging or displays persistent charging alerts.

05

The cooling system will not complete service correctly

Pumps, coolant circulation, air in the system, temperature readings, or module faults interfere with proper operation.

06

Warnings return after codes are cleared

The shop needs help identifying the underlying condition instead of repeatedly clearing alerts or replacing parts.
